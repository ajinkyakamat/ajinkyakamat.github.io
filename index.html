I'm an electrical engineer and roboticist. I enjoy work that crosses boundaries: circuit design, firmware, controls, Linux software, mechanics, and the realities of putting hardware into the field.

I earned a B.Tech. from Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, in 2014. My research and engineering work has included the University of Washington Personal Robotics Lab, Carnegie Mellon University's Biorobotics Lab, and industrial robotics teams.

Early engineering · AVL, 2013

During my internship, I replaced PLC-based closed-loop temperature, fluid, and coolant control for engine test beds with NI LabVIEW software running on Windows CE. AVL subsequently offered the solution as a commercial add-on.
